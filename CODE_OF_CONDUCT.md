# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Agentic Trust Foundation is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and inclusive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, implementations, and evidence.
- Assume good faith while challenging technical claims precisely.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community. Security concerns should be reported privately as described in `SECURITY.md`.
