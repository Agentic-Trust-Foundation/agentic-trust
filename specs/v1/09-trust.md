# 9. Trust

## Status
Normative — ATF Protocol v1.

Trust is contextual evidence and policy about a subject, relationship, issuer, resource, or domain. ATF does not require a universal numeric trust score.

Trust evidence MUST identify its issuer/source and applicable scope and validity. A verifier MUST NOT treat trust evidence as unconditional authorization.

Trust decisions MAY combine multiple independent evidence sources, but the resulting decision MUST remain attributable to the policy and evidence used.
