# 14. Human Approval

## Status
Normative — ATF Protocol v1.

Human approval is an explicit control used when policy requires a human decision before an action proceeds.

An approval MUST be bound to the relevant subject, action/resource scope, context, and validity period. Approval MUST NOT expand authority beyond the applicable delegation and capability constraints.

Approval identity and decision evidence MUST be auditable. Expired, revoked, or context-mismatched approvals MUST NOT authorize execution.
