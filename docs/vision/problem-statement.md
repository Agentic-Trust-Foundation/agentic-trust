# Problem Statement

The agentic internet introduces a new authorization problem: software agents can act on behalf of users and organizations across independent systems, yet existing identity and authorization mechanisms are often designed around human-operated sessions, static applications, or a single administrative domain.

An agent may need to prove who it is, demonstrate who authorized it, show what authority was delegated, operate under constraints, obtain human approval when necessary, and remain revocable and auditable while crossing organizational boundaries.

The core problem is therefore not simply authentication.

> **How can independent systems establish and evaluate delegated authority for autonomous agents in a portable, interoperable, auditable, and least-privileged way?**

Agentic Trust Foundation addresses this problem as an open interoperability layer rather than as a centralized global trust authority.
