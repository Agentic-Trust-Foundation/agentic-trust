# Vision

Agentic Trust Foundation aims to provide an open trust and delegated-authority foundation for an internet in which software agents can safely act across organizational and technical boundaries.

The long-term ecosystem should allow:

- Users to delegate bounded authority to agents.
- Agents to prove relevant identity and authority without exposing unnecessary information.
- Independent organizations and trust domains to establish interoperable trust relationships.
- Services to evaluate rich authorization requests consistently.
- Delegations and capabilities to expire or be revoked.
- High-risk actions to require human approval.
- Decisions and authority chains to remain auditable.
- Multiple implementations and trust providers to interoperate without a mandatory central authority.

The Foundation should become infrastructure that others can build on, not a gatekeeper through which all agent activity must pass.
